PARTICIPANT GUIDE / REVISION 2 / APP V0.3.1

Your first experience with AskAboutEdu

Choose one fictional case and work through it at your own pace. Find a teaching practice, inspect its source, try an AI conversation, and save your own decision. Allow about 25-30 minutes for one case.

Open the application in a new tab ↗

What you can do

  • Search or browse 30 WWC practice guides, then open an individual recommendation and its source passages.
  • Ask AI about a selected recommendation and explore an adaptation for your setting.
  • Edit a plan, choose Try, Adapt, or Set aside, and download your work. No account is required.
This is an early prototype. AI requests sometimes return a source-check or service error. Each case includes a way to continue by reading the source and writing your own plan. Completing that route does not mean the AI request succeeded.

What counts as a useful outcome?

A reasoned decision, a source you checked, and a next step or unresolved question. You do not have to agree with the AI or decide to use the practice. Tell the interviewer where you hesitate or need help.

Scope: this version uses the supplied WWC practice-guide collection. It does not search WWC Intervention Reports, ERIC, or the wider web. Evidence labels refer to original recommendations, not newly proposed activities.

READ ONCE / KEEP THIS GUIDE BESIDE THE APP

Before you begin

Review your data choices

In the application, open About & data. Read the study information and choose whether to check I agree to save this session's interactions. Recording is optional for using the application. If a study-code field appears, use the code supplied by your interviewer.

When you ask AI, your question, setting and relevant sources are sent to the AI service even if interaction recording is off. Use fictional situations and leave out names, student records and identifying details.

Understand the three tabs

  • Explore practices: enter a search, set filters, click Find practices, and select a result.
  • My workspace: read the selected practice, inspect sources, use Ask & explore, and edit My next step. Scroll to see all panels; their positions vary with screen width.
  • About & data: review data choices and use Download my session to keep a copy.

Search results are practices within a guide

The large card title is the recommendation. The smaller text underneath names its guide. Several cards can come from the same guide. In each case, match the large title first, then confirm the guide name underneath.

Typing or changing a filter does not submit a new search. Click Find practices and allow the results to update before selecting a card. Use the matching card even if its position changes.

Two buttons, two different actions

  • Send question sends what is in Your question, with your setting.
  • Help me adapt this practice sends its own adaptation request for the current practice and setting. You do not need to paste another question for that button.

For these cases, use Find practices first. The home-page button Ask AI about my question starts a conversation across the collection instead of following this selected-practice route.

Connection check: if you see Disconnected from the server, the current page cannot submit requests. Preserve any visible work you need, then use Reload or open a new application tab. Reconnection can lose the current session; it does not restore a saved account history.

Case 1: Reading longer words

Set Grade / stage to 4, Your role to Classroom teacher, and Area of practice to All areas.

Build students’ decoding skills so they can read complex multisyllabic words.

GUIDE NAME UNDERNEATH

Providing Reading Interventions for Students in Grades 4-9

GRADE 4 READING / CONVERSATION AND PLAN

Case 1: Ask, adapt and decide

At any AI error: note the message and tell the interviewer. Skip the remaining AI steps and go to step 7 to write your own plan from the practice and sources. Do not ask about a previous answer or plan that never appeared.

4. Add your setting and send one question

In My workspace > Ask & explore, check Current practice, then complete both fields below.

Click Send question once. Wait for an answer or error; do not click repeatedly.

5. If an answer appears, request an adaptation

Read the answer and expand a citation to inspect its quoted passage. Keep the same setting and click Help me adapt this practice once. Wait for the result. Suggested activities are proposals for you to evaluate.

6. If a plan or activity appears, change one constraint

Click Send question once. If there is no previous plan or activity, skip to step 7.

7. Make your own decision and keep it

If a useful Proposed plan appears, expand it and click Use this as my starting draft. Otherwise, click Start my plan and write in My next action. Either way, edit your next step, select Try, Adapt or Set aside, and complete My reason / what I still need to check.

Click Save my plan and look for Saved in this workspace. Click Download plan for a TXT file. Then open About & data > Download my session for a JSON file. Keep both before closing; loading a draft alone does not save it.

CHECKPOINTOutcome to discuss: A decision about one decoding routine, a teacher action, a student action, and an observable sign of how students use the routine.

KEEP YOUR WORK / THERE IS NO REQUIRED RIGHT ANSWER

Save, recover and reflect

If you are writing a plan without an AI draft

Read the practice and at least one supporting source. In My next action, describe your goal, one action, and what you would observe. If the practice does not fit, select Set aside and explain the mismatch or what you need to check. You do not have to invent an activity to finish.

Check your saved files

  • Save my plan: keeps the plan in this active workspace. It does not create a login or a recoverable session history.
  • Download plan: a readable TXT file. Open it and check that your final action and reason are included.
  • Download my session: a JSON file of the current conversation and saved plans. Keep it as an export; this version cannot import it to restore the workspace.
  • Downloads work with recording off. Share files only as agreed in the study. Refreshing, closing or a server restart can lose the active session.

If something does not work

  • Results do not change: check that your text was pasted into the application, click Find practices, and wait for the update. If disconnected, preserve visible work before reloading.
  • The answer did not pass its source checks: no answer was displayed for that request. Note the message, skip further AI requests for this case and continue with step 7. This does not establish whether the practice fits your setting.
  • Service error or question limit: use the same step-7 route. If the app stays on Reading the sources and preparing an answer… for about a minute, tell the interviewer before reloading.
  • No Proposed plan: write your own next step. That section is conditional, not a button that always appears.

Trying another case

Download your work first. New conversation clears the visible chat; it does not reset the AI allowance or change your practice and setting. For the next case, set its search filters, select its matching practice, and replace the setting text.

Questions to discuss with the interviewer

What did you decide, and why? Which source affected your decision? Which parts came from the guide, from an AI proposal, or from your own judgment? Where did you need help? What would make this useful in your work?

Keep the evidence in context. A linked quotation does not guarantee that an AI interpretation is correct. A short lesson or meeting you adapt does not inherit the WWC evidence rating of the original recommendation.
