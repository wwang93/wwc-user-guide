AskAboutEdu Participant Guide

Use this guide to try AskAboutEdu independently: find a practice, examine its source, ask about your setting, and make a plan you can keep. Choose one of the five cases below. You may copy the sample text exactly or change it to reflect a fictional situation of your own.

Open AskAboutEdu ↗

What this version can do

  • Search or browse 30 WWC practice guides across reading and writing, mathematics, classroom behavior, school improvement, and higher education. No account is required.
  • Open a recommendation, its practical steps, evidence label, source passages, and original PDF.
  • Ask AI to explain a selected practice, consider its fit, and propose an adjustment for your setting. Continue with follow-up questions.
  • Edit a next-step plan, choose Try, Adapt, or Set aside, explain your reason, and download your work.

The collection contains 151 recommendations and 14 subparts, so search results are individual practices, not 30 whole-guide results. Three overview documents are available as supporting reading.

For a 25-30 minute activity: spend about 3 minutes getting oriented, 15-20 minutes completing one case, and 5 minutes saving your work and reflecting. The other cases are alternatives, not a requirement to finish all five.

You are testing the application. There is no single correct AI answer or required positive decision. You may disagree with a suggestion, set a practice aside, or ask the interviewer for help. If comfortable, describe what you are looking for and what feels unclear.

QUICK START

Before you begin

1 Open the app and review your data choices

Keep this guide beside the application in a separate tab or window. A laptop or desktop is easiest. Open About & data and read the information provided for your interview. The checkbox I agree to save this session's interactions is optional for using the app; follow the study information and your own choice. If a study-code field appears, use the code provided by the interviewer.

Questions and relevant source passages are sent to the AI service when you ask AI, whether or not interaction recording is on. Use the fictional cases here and leave out names, student records, and other identifying details.

2 Find a practice and inspect its source

Open Explore practices. Paste the case's search text into Describe your question or search a topic, set its grade and role, and leave Area of practice at All areas. Click Find practices. Match the guide title and recommendation below the search results, then click Explore this practice.

In My workspace, read A place to start, How to carry it out, and Fit and evidence. Click a Source button and read Supporting source. Use Open this page in the original PDF to check the passage. PDF page numbers can differ from the page numbers printed in the guide.

3 Ask about the practice and change the setting

Under Ask & explore, paste the case's setting into Your setting and constraints (optional). Paste its first question into Your question and click Send question once. After the answer arrives, click Help me adapt this practice. This button sends its own adaptation request using your selected practice and setting; it does not send custom text left in Your question.

For the final turn, replace the setting with the updated setting, paste the follow-up into Your question, and click Send question. Wait for each answer before submitting another request. The sample route uses three AI requests per case.

4 Read the answer and make your own decision

Look for From the guide, Suggested adaptation, and What to keep in mind when present. Open a citation and compare the claim with its source. The evidence label belongs to the original WWC recommendation; a newly suggested lesson or meeting does not inherit that rating. Save and download your plan using the instructions in each case and in Save and troubleshoot.

Another entry point: Ask AI about my question on the home tab starts an AI conversation across the collection. These cases use Find practices first so you can choose and inspect a specific practice before asking for an adaptation.

SAVING AND TROUBLESHOOTING

Keep your work and share your experience

Save before closing or starting another conversation

  • Save my plan keeps a plan in the current workspace. It is not an account or a guarantee that you can recover it on your next visit.
  • Download plan gives you a readable TXT file with your plan, reason, and sources. Open it to confirm your final edits are included.
  • About & data > Download my session gives you a JSON file containing the current conversation and saved plans. Save your plan first. You can keep this file without understanding its technical structure; this version cannot import it to restore the session.
  • Downloads are available without opting into interaction recording. Share files with the interviewer only as agreed. Keep your copies before refreshing, closing, or leaving the app.

If something does not work

  • No matching practice: use the case's short search phrase, broaden the filters, or browse the guide by title. Each case points to content in this version's collection.
  • Still preparing an answer: wait for the answer or an error; do not click repeatedly. If it remains stuck for about a minute, tell the interviewer before refreshing.
  • Source-check or service error: note the message and the question you asked. You may try one shorter question about the selected practice. If it still fails, continue with the source and plan editor and describe what prevented you from using AI.
  • No Proposed plan section: write or paste a suitable next step from the answer into My next action and edit it. Loading an AI draft does not save it automatically.
  • Question limit reached: continue browsing, checking sources, and editing your plan. Use the remaining-question count shown in the app; New conversation does not reset the allowance.

Trying another case: download the current session before clicking New conversation, which clears the visible chat. Then set the new search filters, select the new practice, and replace the setting text. A new conversation does not automatically change the selected practice or setting.

What this version does not establish

It does not search WWC Intervention Reports, ERIC, or the wider web. AI answers can contain mistakes even when they include verified quotations. Finding a source does not prove that a new activity will improve outcomes in your setting. There is no sign-in history or automatic session restoration.

Be ready to discuss

What did you decide to try, adapt, or set aside, and why? Which source, if any, affected that decision? Where did you hesitate or need help? What would need to change before this could be useful in your own work? An unresolved question or an unsuitable practice is also a useful result to discuss.
